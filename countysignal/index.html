CountySignal

A source-backed rural public-information monitor designed to make important local notices easier to find, understand and verify.

The Illinois-first pilot is centered on Hancock, Adams, McDonough, Warren, Henderson, Schuyler and Brown counties. The goal is to surface useful information buried across official databases, PDFs, utility notices, meeting documents and local sources without turning the project into an opinion or generic news site.

What each notice should answer

What happened? Where? Who may be affected? How time-sensitive is it? Who reported it? When was the underlying source updated? Is it verified, preliminary, disputed, stale, resolved or unknown? What changed since the previous update?
