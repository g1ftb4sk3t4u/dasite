PerfectDay Atlas

Instead of asking only “where should I go?”, PerfectDay Atlas asks the more useful question: when is the best day to go? It ranks upcoming conditions for camping, hiking, fishing, stargazing, photography, biking and other outdoor plans.

See Full Website ↗Back to Labs

What it does

  • Scores upcoming days around the activity you actually care about.
  • Combines weather, wind, precipitation, comfort, clouds, daylight and night-sky conditions.
  • Supports outdoor planning rather than presenting a generic weather forecast.
  • Designed to grow into camping, fishing, astronomy, scenic photography and micro-adventure intelligence.

Why it exists

A forecast tells you what the weather might do. PerfectDay tries to turn that information into a decision: which day gives you the best odds of having the experience you want.
