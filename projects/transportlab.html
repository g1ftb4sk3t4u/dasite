TransportLab

An interactive network-engineering training environment built around the way real outages are actually solved: observe state, form a hypothesis, test it, follow the path, and prove the failure domain.

See Full Website ↗Back to Labs

What it covers

  • OSPF pathing and cost behavior.
  • MPLS/LDP state and pseudowire troubleshooting.
  • Optics, interface counters and transport fault isolation.
  • Multi-vendor thinking across MikroTik, Juniper, Nokia and Cisco-style workflows.
  • Scenario-driven troubleshooting instead of memorizing disconnected commands.

Lab philosophy

observe → isolate → verify → document

The point is not to simulate every vendor command perfectly. It is to teach the reasoning pattern that transfers between platforms and makes real transport troubleshooting faster.
