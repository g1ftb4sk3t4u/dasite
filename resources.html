AI

Cybersecurity

Defense
  • OWASP — Application-security projects, testing guidance and reference material.

Networking & Internet Engineering

Packets
  • IETF — Working groups, meetings and the standards process behind Internet protocols.
  • RFC Editor — The canonical archive for RFCs.
  • RIPE NCC — Routing, addressing, measurement and Internet-operations resources.
  • Hurricane Electric BGP Toolkit — Practical public ASN, prefix and peering lookup.

  • FCC Wireless — U.S. spectrum and wireless-service reference.
